# Decisions

Append-only. Add at the bottom. Never edit an entry after the fact, and never delete one.
When a decision changes, write a new entry that supersedes the old one and say so. The old
entry stays, wrong, forever, because the record of what you believed and when is the thing
that makes this trustworthy.

The resolution rule is mechanical so that a reader in a hurry still gets it right: **the
highest-numbered entry on a topic wins.** No entry needs to claim precedence, because
precedence lives in the structure rather than in the text. A ledger is the only document
type where age is not a defect, since age is the whole indexing scheme.

Mark each entry as a decision by a person or by whoever is doing the building, so a reader
can tell which ones are open to argument.

**D-002 2026-01-04 [BUILD] - A decision somebody made while working.**
State the decision, then the reason. The reason is the part that stops somebody re-deriving
it badly in six months.

**D-003 2026-02-11 [PERSON] - Supersedes D-002.**
The situation changed. Here is what is true now and why it differs. D-002 stays above,
wrong, on purpose.
